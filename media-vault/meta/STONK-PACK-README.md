# The Pairing Department — ZYCLOPS community pack

Six original ZYCLOPS files inspired by Stonk's “pair anything” theme: three jokes, two reactions and one blank caption template.

Browse: https://zyclops.xyz/media-vault/?pack=stonk#library

Download the six-file pack: https://zyclops.xyz/media-vault/zyclops-stonk-community-pack-v1.zip

## Make it yours

- Download an image and write your own caption. These files are free to share and remix for community memes.
- For the blank template, add your two paired things to the empty cards and put your headline in the space above. Short captions read best on a phone.
- Share your version with @ZYCLOPSSOl. Keep the ZYCLOPS footer when practical so others can find the original files.
- `captions.json` in the ZIP includes suggested captions, image descriptions and the six asset records. Image paths are relative to the ZIP root.

This is independent community artwork by ZYCLOPS. It is not an official Stonk release or an endorsement by Stonk. The pairing jokes describe fictional situations; ZYCLOPS itself trades against SOL. Images do not describe token returns or technical features.

## Character

One central ivory-and-gold eye, one solid gold brow, charcoal face, short beard and black hoodie. Keep the familiar face when remixing. The deliberately blank template has `requires_caption: true` in the inventory; posting bots should wait for a completed captioned version rather than publish it automatically.
