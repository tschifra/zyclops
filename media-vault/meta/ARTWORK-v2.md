# ZYCLOPS media vault — volume 2

Created using the built-in image generation tool with the established ZYCLOPS illustration as a mascot/style reference. The character keeps one central golden eye, an angular beard, charcoal ink and warm gold accents. Exact submitted prompts are preserved in `prompts-v2.json`; source PNGs remain in the original generation output directory.

## New artwork

Eight original holder illustrations: Night watch, The blind spot, Golden sentinel, Moonwalk, After the rain, The forge, Campfire and Redacted world. These have no meme headline, with a small ZYCLOPS / $ZYCL footer. Six newly generated alternatives expand the BASED, TOUCH GRASS, WEN MOON, WAGMI, NGMI and SER reactions. The ten earlier generated scenes are already included in volume 1 and are not duplicated as new stills.

## Motion method

Four illustrated scene loops add localized movement: coffee steam, a bicycle-pump action, a blinking eye behind a curtain, and flickering flames with sparks. Four motion posters use a gentle looping camera move across the holder artwork. Three branded character loops adapt the existing ZYCLOPS animated stickers. These are locally rendered animations of illustrations, not image-to-video model footage. Sound is intentionally absent.

Delivery: 1200px square JPEG; 720px square H.264 MP4, 30 fps, six seconds, YUV420P and fast-start; seven 480px square GIF alternatives, 12 fps. MP4 is the preferred video version. GIF is a smaller-format alternate for reactions.

All volume 1 media files, asset IDs and media URLs are preserved unchanged. New IDs use a versioned prefix. The bot manifest groups related stills and animations with `creative_id` to support repeat avoidance.
